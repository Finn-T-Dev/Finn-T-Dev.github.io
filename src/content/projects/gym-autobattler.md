# Gym-Themed Autobattler Prototype

WIP of my prototype for a gym-themed autobattler game. 

## Overview
An autobattler that pits you (the player) against fierce athletes in the gym in pursuit of life's paramount achievement: Gym Alpha. Choose from a diverse range of playable characters and set off on your journey to claim the ultimate prize. You'll need to prepare, plan and perform if you want to make it through all 8 workouts. Plan your routine from the warm-up to the top set, learn new exercises from gym legends and purchase supplements from questionable vendors to enhance your gains. Your enemies will be doing whatever it takes, will you?
